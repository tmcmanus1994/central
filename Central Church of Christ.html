Welcome to Central Church of Christ in Downtown Little Rock, AR! Our mission is to follow Jesus together as a diverse congregation representing our community. Join us for weekly classes, worship on Sundays at 10:15 a.m (Classes at 9am) and classes on Wednesdays at 6:30 p.m. Discover a community striving to walk in the footsteps of Jesus.
