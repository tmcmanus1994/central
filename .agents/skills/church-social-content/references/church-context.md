## Ministries & Programs

### Outreach (Community-Facing)
- **Kids' Closet** — Major outreach. Serves ~50-60 families weekly with children's clothing/supplies. Run by Lacey. Volunteers needed.
- **Canvas Meal** — Wednesday evening meal served to neighbors. Meet at church 4:30, go down the street together in the church van. Cook Tuesday night (Justin Beringer's lasagna).
- **Mentoring Program** — Tuesday afternoons, one-on-one with students. Run by Eric & Landon. Need ~46 mentors (have 23).
- **Galant Times** — Annual women's event celebrating Kids' Closet moms. Dinner, games, community. February. Childcare needed because teens are at Winter Fest.
- **Blessing Bags** — Kids raise money through bake sales to pack bags for under-resourced neighbors. Keep in your car to hand out.
- **SNAP Relief** — Emergency grocery/meal assistance (spent ~$10K in Nov 2025)
- **MLK Events** — Community events around Martin Luther King Day
- **Toy Store** — Christmas outreach
- **Glacier Ministry** — Hispanic service across the street on Sunday afternoons (new in 2025)

### In-Reach (Congregation-Facing)
- **Sunday Classes** — 9:00 AM. Currently studying Genesis.
- **Wednesday Night Classes** — 6:30 PM. Steven's class (finishing Luke, moving to new material). Prayer pods class. Helping Hands. New prayer class.
- **Prayer Pods** — Men's small prayer groups. Meet at various times. Wednesday night option at 6:30. Coordinated by Josh Ward.
- **Taste Buds** — Men's lunch. Thursdays at noon in the gym. Simple meal + 10 min challenge. Run by Steven/Shannon.
- **Helping Hands** — Wednesday night group (8-12 people). Volunteer in Kids' Closet, pray over KC families. Great entry point.
- **Women's Retreat** — March 13-15, 2026 at Corin Reed Christian Camp. ~30 min south of Little Rock.
- **Valentine's Brunch** — Saturday event. Men cook/serve, ladies enjoy. Childcare provided.

### Youth

## The Central Vibe in One Paragraph

Central is a downtown Little Rock church where a former missionary kid from Tennessee cracks jokes about burying people in the courtyard, a youth minister loses his AirPods and turns it into a sermon illustration, kids pray for "people under a rug at home," the congregation passes a microphone during prayer time so everyone can share, the budget is presented with full transparency every January, and a homeless woman named Jean found her people before she died. It's real. It's messy. It's diverse. It's funny. It's holy. Steven calls it "this quirky little church downtown" and that's exactly right.
