# Central Church of Christ — Content Calendar & Seasonal Guide

## Weekly Content Rhythm

| Day | Post Type | Platform Focus | Time to Post |
|-----|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| Sunday AM | Teaser (if pre-service) | FB + IG + Stories | 7:30-8:30 AM |
| Sunday PM | Recap + Quote | FB + IG + Stories | 1:00-3:00 PM |
| Monday | Key Quote Graphic | FB + IG | 11:00 AM-1:00 PM |
| Tuesday | Reflection Question | FB + IG | 11:00 AM-1:00 PM |

| Thursday | Practical Application | FB + IG | 11:00 AM-1:00 PM |
| Friday | Community/Event Spotlight | FB + IG | 1:00-4:00 PM |
| Saturday | Sunday Invitation | FB + IG + Stories | 5:00-7:00 PM |

## Weekly Batching Workflow (60-90 min)

### After Sunday Service
1. Read/listen to the sermon transcript (15 min)
2. Extract: main message, key quotes, scripture, action steps (10 min)
3. Run the `/church-social-content` skill with the transcript
4. Review and personalize the 7 generated posts (15 min)
5. Create or source images/graphics (15 min)
6. Schedule all posts for the week (10 min)
7. Leave Friday flexible for real-time event content

### Tools for Scheduling
- **Facebook Business Suite** (free — schedules FB + IG together)
- **Later** or **Buffer** (if you want more features)
- **Canva** (for quick graphics — use a Central template)

## Seasonal & Church Calendar Content

### January
- New Year: "Fresh starts. New mercies. Let's go, 2026."
- Theme: New beginnings, resolutions through faith
- MLK Day: Celebrate diversity, justice, and Central's commitment to inclusion

### February
- Valentine's/Love: Love as action (1 Corinthians 13 content)
- Black History Month: Celebrate diversity at Central and in our community

### March
- Lent (if observed): Reflective, contemplative content
- Spring: Renewal themes, new growth

### April
- Easter: THIS IS THE BIG ONE
  - 4-week lead-up campaign
  - Invite-a-friend posts
  - "Who are you bringing to Easter?" engagement
  - Easter Sunday photo dump + celebration recap
  - Special service details and times

### May
- Mother's Day: Celebrate moms in the congregation
- End of school year: Family content, summer preview

### June
- Father's Day: Celebrate dads
- Summer series launch (if applicable)
- VBS or summer programs

### July
- Independence Day: Community celebration (not political)
- Summer fellowship events

### August
- Back to School: Blessing of students, backpack drives
- Fall kickoff teasers

### September
- Fall series launch
- Small group sign-ups
- Community service events

### October
- Fall festival / Trunk or Treat (if applicable)
- Community outreach emphasis

### November
- Thanksgiving: Gratitude series
- "What are you thankful for?" engagement posts
- Giving/generosity theme

### December
- Advent: Weekly candle/theme posts
- Christmas Eve service promotion (start early!)
- "Christmas at Central" campaign
- Year-in-review post (photos, milestones, baptisms, growth)
- New Year's Eve: Reflection + anticipation

## Event Promotion Timeline

### 3+ Weeks Out
- Save-the-date announcement
- Add to Stories highlights

### 2 Weeks Out
- Details post (what, when, where, who it's for)
- Start countdown in Stories

### 1 Week Out
- Reminder post with excitement
- "Tag someone you're bringing"
- Stories countdown sticker

### Day Before
- "Tomorrow!" reminder
- Logistics (parking, what to bring, etc.)

### Day Of
- Morning Stories: "It's happening today!"
- Live Stories during event (if appropriate)

### Day After
- Recap post with photos
- Thank-you to volunteers and attendees
- "Missed it? Here's what happened"

## Metrics to Track Monthly

### Engagement
- Comments per post (most important — means people care)
- Shares (means people want others to see it)
- Saves on Instagram (means it's valuable enough to revisit)

### Growth
- New followers per month
- Profile visits
- Website clicks from social

### Content Performance
- Top 3 posts of the month (what worked and why)
- Bottom 3 posts (what to adjust)
- Best posting day/time (from platform analytics)

### Qualitative
- Did anyone mention seeing us on social media?
- Did any visitors say they found us through Facebook/Instagram?
- Are members sharing our posts?

## Content Ideas When You're Stuck

1. Repost a quote graphic from a previous week that performed well
2. Share a scripture with a one-line reflection
3. Post a photo from a past event with a memory caption
4. Ask a simple question: "What song has been stuck in your head from Sunday?"
5. Share a local Little Rock event or cause Central supports
6. Post the podcast link with a teaser quote
7. "This week at Central" quick schedule rundown
8. Behind-the-scenes photo (even a messy one — keeps it real)
9. Re-share a member's post about Central (with permission)
10. "First time visiting?" info post (parking, dress code, what to expect)
