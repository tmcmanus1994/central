# Central Church of Christ — Post Templates

Ready-to-adapt templates for each day of the weekly content calendar. Replace bracketed text with sermon-specific content.

## TUESDAY — Reflection / Question

```
Facebook:
Sunday, Steven asked us to think about [concept].

Here's what we're sitting with this week: [reframe as a personal question].

What would your answer be?

Instagram:
Honest question:

[Thought-provoking question tied to Sunday's message]

Drop your answer below.

#FollowJesusTogether #CentralChurchLR #FaithQuestions #GrowTogether #ChurchFamily
[Image: Simple text graphic with the question, or a candid photo]
```

### Question Bank (Adapt to Sermon)
- "What's one thing you'd do differently if you really believed [sermon theme]?"
- "When was the last time [sermon topic] showed up in your everyday life?"
- "If someone asked you what [concept] means — how would you explain it?"
- "What's the hardest part about [applying sermon takeaway]?"

## BONUS — Stories Templates

### Sunday Morning Story Sequence
1. "Good morning, Central!" (photo of setup or empty seats)
2. "We're getting ready for you" (worship team warmup, behind scenes)
3. "[Sermon title] starts in 30 min" (countdown sticker)
4. Worship moment (15-sec video clip)
5. "See you next week!" (post-service fellowship photo)

### Mid-Week Story
1. Poll: "How's your week going?" (scale or emoji options)
2. Encouragement tied to Sunday's message
3. "Classes tonight at 6:30" (with location/details)

### Event Countdown
1. "3 days until [event]!" (countdown sticker)
2. "Here's what to expect..." (details)
3. Day-of: "It's happening TODAY" (reminder)
4. Post-event: Recap photos/video

## Content That Always Works

These are evergreen post ideas you can pull from anytime:

- "There's room for you here." (with welcoming photo)
- Scripture of the day + one-line reflection
- "This week at Central..." (quick rundown of what's happening)
- Throwback photos from past events
- "Why I come to Central" member quote (with permission)
- Local Little Rock content (supporting the community)
- Holiday-specific posts (not generic — make them Central)
- New visitor FAQ: "What to expect your first Sunday"
- Parking/directions/what-to-wear info for newcomers
