# Central Church of Christ — Voice & Tone Guide

## Our Voice (Consistent)

Central's voice stays the same across every piece of content. We are:

### Warm
We talk to people, not at them. Every post should feel like a friend saying, "Hey, you should know about this."

**Do:** "We'd love to see you Sunday."
**Don't:** "You are cordially invited to attend our worship assembly."

### Real
We don't hide behind church-speak. We acknowledge that life is messy, faith is a journey, and nobody has it all figured out.

**Do:** "Some weeks are hard. That's okay. You don't have to have it together to show up."
**Don't:** "Let go and let God! He has a plan for your life!"

### Inclusive
Our congregation is diverse — racially, economically, generationally — and our content reflects that. Everyone should see themselves in what we post.

**Do:** "There's a seat for you here."
**Don't:** "Come join our church family!" (implies you're not already welcome)

### Hopeful
We lead with hope, not guilt. We point to what's possible through Jesus, not what's wrong with people.

**Do:** "Following Jesus changes how we see everything — even Monday mornings."
**Don't:** "Are you living a life that honors God? Come hear how."

## Our Tone (Shifts by Context)

While voice stays consistent, tone adapts:

### Sunday Recap / Sermon Content
- **Tone:** Reflective, inspiring, conversational
- **Energy:** Calm confidence
- "Steven reminded us Sunday that love isn't a feeling — it's a decision we make every single day."

### Event Promotion
- **Tone:** Excited, inviting, casual
- **Energy:** Warm enthusiasm
- "This Saturday. Pancakes. Fellowship. No agenda — just good people and good food. You in?"

### Wednesday Reminder
- **Tone:** Encouraging, gentle nudge
- **Energy:** Supportive
- "Mid-week check-in: How's your week going? We're gathering tonight at 6:30 if you need a reset."

### Devotional / Encouragement
- **Tone:** Gentle, grounding, personal
- **Energy:** Quiet strength
- "You don't have to earn God's love. You already have it. Sit with that today."

### Community Spotlight
- **Tone:** Celebratory, grateful
- **Energy:** Joyful
- "Shoutout to our volunteers who showed up early to make Sunday happen. You are seen and appreciated."

## Language Do's and Don'ts

### Words We Use
| Instead of... | We say... |
|---------------|-----------|
| Fellowship | Community, gathering, hanging out |
| Congregation | Church family, our people, Central |
| Worship assembly | Sunday worship, our gathering |
| Sanctification | Growing in faith, becoming more like Jesus |
| Evangelism | Sharing our faith, inviting people in |
| Outreach | Serving our neighbors, showing up for our city |
| Devotional time | Time with God, quiet time, prayer time |
| Non-believers | People who are exploring faith, seekers, guests |
| Backsliding | Struggling, going through it |
| Convicted | Challenged, moved, inspired |

### Phrases That Feel Like Us
- "Follow Jesus together"
- "There's room for you here"
- "Come as you are"
- "Growing God's Kingdom one person at a time"
- "Downtown Little Rock. Doors wide open."
- "Real people. Real faith. Real community."
- "We don't have it all figured out — but we're figuring it out together."

### Phrases That Don't Feel Like Us
- "Are you saved?"
- "God has a plan for your life" (too cliche)
- "We are blessed and highly favored"
- "Doing life together" (overused)
- "On fire for God"
- "Prayer warriors"
- Anything that sounds like a bumper sticker

## Caption Length Guidelines

### Facebook
- **Ideal:** 2-4 sentences (40-80 words)
- **Max:** 100 words before people scroll past
- **Structure:** Hook → Value → CTA

### Instagram
- **Ideal:** 1-3 sentences (20-50 words) above the fold
- **Extended:** Can go longer if the content is a story/devotional — but front-load the hook
- **Hashtags:** 5-10 at the end, separated by line break

### Stories
- **Text overlay:** 5-10 words max
- **Stickers:** Use polls, questions, countdowns for engagement
- **Frequency:** 3-5 Stories on event days, 1-2 on normal days

## Image & Visual Direction

### Photography Style
- Natural light, candid moments
- Show diversity in every group photo
- Worship, fellowship, service — not just the stage
- Smiling faces, real moments, not posed

### Graphics Style
- Clean, modern, minimal
- Use consistent brand colors and fonts (reference church brand kit if available)
- Scripture quotes: White text on muted/warm background
- Event graphics: Clear date/time/place, not cluttered

### Video
- Sermon clips: 30-60 seconds max for Reels
- Talking head: Minister or member sharing a thought (vertical, casual)
- B-roll: Worship moments, community, downtown Little Rock
